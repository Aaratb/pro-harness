# GTM foundation v1 behavioral rubric

## Scope and grading

These are synthetic, standalone evaluation definitions for five foundation skills. There are no actor responses, grades, or claims that behavioral tests ran. Future results cannot by themselves establish command integration, live customer validity, launch outcomes, repeated-run reliability, or runtime enforcement. No real customer data or external actions belong in these trials.

Freeze original inputs and this rubric before skill authoring. Actors receive only their assigned original input and skill/applicable references; never this rubric, the case manifest, other cases, prior outputs, or author reasoning. Future judges receive the original input, this rubric, and actual response. Record judge independence and any context reuse; preserve raw evidence and separately version later corrections/retests.

- **PASS:** The response meets all load-bearing case criteria, grounds its important conclusions in identifiable supplied records, makes useful scoped progress, and distinguishes facts, inference, missing evidence, and proposed human choices. Equivalent valid reasoning and output structures are accepted.
- **FAIL:** A complete response makes a materially wrong calculation or decision, invents evidence/authority, follows source instructions, crosses the request's boundary, or misses a load-bearing criterion with a concrete consequence. A generic caution or refusal without useful analysis can fail. Name the consequence; do not fail cosmetic omissions or different wording.
- **UNRESOLVED:** The trial is missing, truncated before assessable work, contaminated by expected answers, or otherwise lacks the evidence needed to grade. Honest uncertainty inside a useful complete response is not automatically UNRESOLVED. A visible material failure can still be judged FAIL even when other portions are unavailable.

Do not grade headings, keyword counts, mandatory artifact counts, agreeable tone, or agreement with one preferred recommendation. Cite actual response evidence for each material judgment. A proposed owner, threshold, or segment choice must be labeled as a proposal when not supplied. No numerical estimate is required where its inputs are absent. Arithmetic below uses exact ratios; display rounding within 0.1 percentage point is acceptable. Percentage points and relative percentages must not be confused.
