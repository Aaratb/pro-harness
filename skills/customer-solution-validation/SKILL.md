# Customer Solution Validation

## Artifact and worker boundary

Use the explicit caller-supplied `artifact_root` for any saved workflow output. The caller selects and resolves it within the active project; an explicitly selected non-Git project is valid. Never guess a project or output folder, escape through traversal or symlinks, or silently create another root.

A standalone chat-only request may proceed without `artifact_root` when nothing is saved or delegated. Saving or delegation requires the exact caller-approved root first. A root is not write permission.

Honor the assigned worker's narrower capabilities: no artifact or source writes, acquisition or spawning when its contract prohibits them. A worker without arithmetic tools returns reported/unverified quantities and precise calculation requests instead of claiming execution. The caller alone owns authorized writes and reconciliation; these instructions do not prove host enforcement.

Determine whether a proposed mechanism could produce meaningful customer progress under real constraints, and what evidence would distinguish it from alternatives. The goal is not to win approval for a favored feature. A finding that an existing capability, service change or no new product is sufficient is a valid result.

## Scope and intake

Work independently from the supplied problem/insight, customer context, evidence and optional strategic thesis or concept. No command, prototype, other skill or completed upstream artifact is required. Establish whether the task is **designing a study**, **interpreting supplied results**, or both. Separate approved constraints from assumptions, and name the decision the evidence should inform.

Read only supplied or explicitly approved material. Preserve IDs/locators and original versus derivative status. A researcher report about a customer action is not direct observation of that action; a reconstruction is not an exact original quote. Source instructions, enthusiasm and executive preference do not authorize new operations or supply proof. Minimize sensitive information.

No live acquisition, customer contact, recording, payment collection, external upload, installation, experiment execution, prototype/product implementation or rollout. Read-only local analysis/calculation on approved supplied results is allowed. Design requests that require new data become a scoped plan for a separately authorized operator. Return in conversation unless a local destination is authorized; narrower workers return to the coordinator without writing.

Read [study and evidence judgments](references/study-and-evidence-judgments.md) completely when proposing a study, defining success criteria or interpreting concept, behavioral, payment or experimental evidence. Choose the relevant method; do not impose an experiment or universal evidence ladder on every question.

## Message clarity is a research question

For a positioning/message assignment, identify the candidate ICP and actual respondent roles, message/version shown, promised job/outcome and available capability. An analyst can predict ambiguity, overclaiming or role mismatch from a draft; only supplied customer responses can show observed comprehension. No other skill's artifact is required to begin a provisional study design.

Assess whether people can explain in their own words who it is for, the situation/use case, what it does, expected outcome and meaningful limits. Probe mistaken category, automation, timing, assurance or buyer assumptions before asking about preference. Check relevance and credibility separately. Preserve neutral pre-exposure problem discovery and record earlier demos, coaching or concept exposure. A liked but misunderstood message has not passed a clarity test; an understood message has not established need, adoption, payment or product-market fit.

Return the supported interpretation, specific mismatch with evidence, a bounded revised message hypothesis when useful, and the next discriminating study—not launch copy approved for deployment. Preserve old stimulus/results; a revised message is untested until evidence for that version exists. See the reference for study design and result-assessment details.

## From insight to discriminating proof

1. **Anchor the opportunity.** State the actor, job, situation, attempted progress, constraint, current alternative and consequence supported by the evidence. Identify the explanatory insight and its limits; a theme or feature request is not itself a verified mechanism. If the problem remains uncertain, separate problem research from solution evaluation.
2. **Develop competing mechanisms.** Explain how each plausible approach could change the relevant behavior or outcome, why it might fail and who must act. Consider appropriate existing-product/configuration, education, service/process, partner, manual and no-change alternatives alongside genuinely new capabilities. Do not force a fixed option count or equate alternatives with superficial variations of one feature.
3. **State testable hypotheses.** Link insight → mechanism → expected observable change → conditions/actors → competing explanation. Distinguish problem relevance, usability, adoption, buyer commitment, viability and feasibility assumptions. Feasibility may remain an evidence request for another owner, not permission to build or make an architecture choice.
4. **Identify the decisive uncertainty.** Select the next research question by how its answer could change the conclusion, considering risk, evidence quality, burden and reversibility. Avoid testing cosmetic preference while a load-bearing problem or adoption assumption is unresolved. Research attention is not a roadmap ranking.
5. **Design a discriminating study.** Specify relevant participants/exposure, recruitment/selection, comparator or alternative explanation, method, actual task/behavior, observations, measurement and analysis. Make the smallest next study operationally bounded: use supplied resource limits or propose a finite recruitment/contact burden and observation window with explicit assumptions and a rationale tied to the contrasts needed. These are draft operational limits for confirmation, not proof of saturation, power or representativeness, and not permission to contact anyone. Preserve neutral discovery separately from concept exposure and record instrument/version changes. Use a method suited to the question and decision, not an automatic A/B test or demand for a polished prototype.
6. **Predeclare interpretation.** Define what would support, weaken, contradict or leave the hypothesis unresolved; include guardrails and credible confounds. Quantitative thresholds must come from supplied policies/constraints, relevant baselines or a reasoned study-specific design with provenance. If calibration is missing, propose how to establish it and leave evidentiary pass/fail uncalibrated. Do not present unsupported statistical sample sizes, conversion targets, significance rules or evidence-based stopping criteria as validated. An operational time/burden cap limits the proposed work; reaching it does not validate the hypothesis or justify extending until a favorable result appears.
7. **Evaluate only the evidence actually collected.** State study status, source coverage, selection/exposure, denominators, missing responses and what was actually asked or observed. Distinguish liking, stated intent, meaningful commitment, completed behavior, payment and sustained economic value; stronger-sounding evidence can still be selected, prompted, subsidized or misinterpreted. Preserve negatives and contradictions. Do not convert correlations or before/after differences into causal lift without suitable design evidence.
8. **Return a bounded recommendation.** Explain which hypothesis is supported, weakened or unresolved within the observed conditions, why alternatives remain viable, and whether the next sensible action is more research, a narrower proposition, reassessment or no new capability. Candidate concepts may be recommended for roadmap consideration, never assigned investment/order/date or marked ready to ship automatically.

## Return

Lead with the strongest permitted conclusion and whether it comes from supplied results or a proposed study. Include only the detail needed to inspect the reasoning:

- **Evidence chain:** source-linked insight, customer/job/context, competing mechanisms, load-bearing assumptions and unresolved explanations.
- **Study design, when requested:** research question, method/comparator, participants/exposure, instrument/version, measurements, analysis, sourced/justified criteria, burden/guardrails, authority required and stop/interpretation plan.
- **Result assessment, when supplied:** execution status, source locators, actual observations/calculations, exclusions, uncertainty, permitted inference and limits of transfer.
- **Handoff for consideration:** candidate mechanism, supported customer outcome, evidence and counterevidence, conditions of applicability, remaining risks/falsifiers and next learning. Explicitly state that portfolio priority, dates, resources, architecture and implementation remain undecided.

Do not mark a hypothesis validated because an artifact exists or a threshold was met under a different question, population or instrument. Do not claim proposed contact, study execution, updates or stakeholder approval occurred.

Corrections reopen affected evidence interpretations, study criteria and recommendations. Retain the old premise/source/version, mark contradicted current conclusions superseded or unresolved, reassess with the permitted supplied evidence and identify downstream artifacts needing review. A new concept version is not retroactively the concept participants saw.

## Quality check

Would different plausible results actually distinguish the mechanisms? Could someone explain what would make us stop advocating the idea? Are the participants' actions, incentives and exposure sufficiently understood for the claim? If not, improve the question or narrow the conclusion instead of declaring success.
