# Conditional Audience Rules and Worked Cases

Use the applicable branch below when the audience recommendation depends on more than a straightforward fit condition. These examples illustrate judgment, not a universal scoring system or an operational targeting procedure.

## Build an inspectable rule without inventing precision

Connect each material rule to the value mechanism and the supplied capability. Useful rule language names a situation and observable condition: “Teams handling changed assignments through this supported workflow, whose receiving crews can use the offered acknowledgment route.” It is stronger than “digitally mature teams” unless maturity has an evidenced definition relevant to the job.

Distinguish:

- **Necessary condition:** without it, the current offer cannot produce the intended progress in this situation.
- **Enabling condition:** it makes adoption or delivery more plausible but is not established as universally required.
- **Proxy:** a field that may approximate a condition; state the evidence and mismatch risk.
- **Operating bound:** a supplied capacity, service promise or permitted launch limit, not a claim about inherent customer fit.
- **Unresolved condition:** a consequential question requiring confirmation; do not convert it into failure.

An employee count can be descriptive or a practical supplied constraint, but a sample containing only larger firms does not justify a minimum headcount. Similarly, a supported region may be an actual operating bound; inventing regional exclusions from language, nationality or assumptions about willingness to pay is not analysis.

A rule can be provisional and still guide a next step. State what observable fact would support it, what would contradict it and what decision would then change. When a threshold is supplied, preserve its origin and purpose; do not silently reuse a research-screening threshold as a commercial qualification rule.

## Avoid collapsing dimensions into a winner score

Two candidates can have different strengths without being directly rankable. A well-understood job with unknown contact permission is not necessarily worse fit than a reachable but weakly understood job. An entitled installed account with no exposure is not a failed adopter. A customer outside a current rollout's technical eligibility can still strengthen the underlying fit thesis for a future offer.

If comparison helps, use separate columns and state how each condition changes the recommendation. Do not add arbitrary points and claim the highest total is the best audience. Distinguish prioritizing an advisory starting hypothesis from approving which accounts a company will commercially target.

Useful dispositions include:

- Candidate for the specified offer, subject to an unresolved permission or operational check.
- Candidate only for an explicitly assisted configuration, not current self-service.
- Cannot adopt the current configuration because a named prerequisite fails.
- Should retain the current arrangement because the supplied switching tradeoff or continuity risk is unfavorable.
- Material condition unknown; preserve the case and identify the exact discriminating check.

Use only dispositions needed by the case. Do not create an exhaustive taxonomy for its own sake.

## Installed-base launches and migrations

Existing users can occupy different states: access without awareness, first attempts, repeat value, dependence on a legacy capability, partially migrated work, or intentional non-use. Analyze the change relative to each supported state instead of treating everyone as an acquisition prospect.

Trace the existing job and what the transition changes: retained or missing capability, data continuity, integrations, permissions, training, support and service commitments. A current release can solve an old obstacle while leaving migration cost or a different prerequisite unresolved. An entitlement scheduled for a future date is not active today.

Treat “cannot migrate” and “should not migrate yet” differently. The former may concern a missing technical capability; the latter may concern a satisfactory current arrangement, timing or an avoidable burden. Both can coexist with a long-term fit hypothesis. Neither authorizes sunsetting the old arrangement, granting access or choosing a roadmap repair.

A supplied correction should update the current analysis without rewriting history. If offline support appeared in version 3, a version-2 failed-adoption episode remains historically valid. Reconsider the present eligibility rule and the dependent claims; do not assert that the previously blocked account has now adopted or received value.

## Assisted pilots and partner capacity

Evaluate the combined offer that can actually be delivered. An assisted pilot is not inherently illegitimate or evidence of poor fit. It may be a sensible way to learn about a valuable job when support is deliberately bounded. Make the assistance visible so the team does not mistake service work for product readiness.

The conditional audience should match the available support: the relevant workflow, required data permissions, who performs the manual work, the supplied capacity limit, the customer burden and the offered outcome. If capacity or permission is unknown, propose the confirmation that would make the candidate actionable; do not invent a staffing commitment or stop at an empty unknown label.

For partner-dependent offers, distinguish commercial agreement, trained staff, available capacity and delivered service. A partner's availability in one place or period does not establish another. The recommendation should state the supported service condition and the observation that would reverse it, such as failed handoffs or a withdrawn capacity commitment.

A successful assisted case can support an assisted-offer hypothesis within those conditions. Expansion into unaided or broader delivery requires new evidence about the changed configuration. Do not relabel a pilot outcome as scalable product-market fit or authorize exposure expansion.

## Worked judgment example: a scheduling-product launch

The following packet and cases are fictional teaching material, not real customer findings or a prescribed cohort size.

**Supplied context.** A scheduling product's current version supports browser dispatch and text acknowledgment of changed assignments. It does not support receiving changes without connectivity. The existing provisional fit thesis concerns teams whose current handoffs sometimes fail when assignments change. A launch owner is considering self-service availability plus a separately staffed assisted pilot. The packet provides no market prevalence study or authorization to contact accounts.

| Supplied case | Evidence in the packet | Advisory interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account A | Its coordinator describes failed change handoffs. Its plan is entitled, the supported workflow is eligible, and it has not yet seen this version. Commercial contact permission is unspecified. | A plausible candidate situation for this offer. Do not call it a failed adopter or put it in a send-ready list; exposure and permission remain distinct. |
| Account B | Field crews must receive assignments while disconnected. The current product cannot do that. A future roadmap note proposes offline support. | Cannot adopt this current configuration for that job. Preserve future conditional fit and the valuable contradictory case; a roadmap note is not a current capability or approved priority. |
| Account C | It uses the older workflow successfully, and the owner reports no relevant unmet handoff need. Switching would require retraining during an ongoing project. | No supported reason to migrate now under these conditions. Keeping the current arrangement is a legitimate alternative, not resistance to be overcome by more messages. |
| Account D | Its job resembles A's, but it needs manual import assistance. A supplied staffing note reserves support for a bounded pilot; the data-transfer permission needed for the import has not been established. | Conditional candidate for the assisted offer, not self-service. Confirm the named import permission and support scope before any separate execution decision. Do not infer permission from the staffing note. |
| Account E | A partner serves the relevant area, but the only readiness evidence is a signed partnership document. No fulfillment schedule is supplied. | Underlying fit may be plausible; the partner-dependent promise is not operationally supported yet. Request the relevant service-capacity confirmation rather than labeling the customer bad fit. |

**Recommendation.** Provisionally favor the situation represented by A for the self-service hypothesis: a documented change-handoff job compatible with the current acknowledgment workflow. D represents a different, explicitly assisted hypothesis. This does not select A or D for enrollment, establish the frequency of these situations, or show that either receives repeat value. B and C should not be folded into the same immediate migration audience. E's constraint is delivery readiness, not demonstrated customer mismatch.

**Observable rule.** “A current change-handoff problem, a supported receiving workflow and the ability to use the offered acknowledgment route” is a candidate value/adoption rule. Active entitlement, applicable eligibility, actual exposure, contact permission and support capacity are separately evaluated states. Do not replace that rule with “all large field-service companies” or invent a cutoff from the cases.

**Rival explanation.** Apparent benefit may come from a coordinator's extra manual attention rather than the acknowledgment capability. Another rival is that the current calendar process already meets the job when followed consistently. These possibilities make a comparison of normal change-handoff episodes more useful than a broad enthusiasm survey.

**Next proof.** Propose a bounded comparison of similar changed-assignment jobs using the current alternative and the offered configuration, tracking actual receipt, the intended service outcome and assistance required. Correct receipt with less total burden would strengthen the mechanism. Continued service failure despite receipt would challenge the proposed value chain. Success requiring the pilot employee would narrow the audience–offer claim to the assisted configuration. This is a proposed observation design, not permission to expose customers or operate the pilot.

**Reversal conditions.** Reopen the initial rule if the relevant job is rarely encountered, the receiving workflow cannot be used, the existing alternative reliably meets the need at lower burden, or the assumed support/partner capacity disappears. Do not move the intended outcome to “messages sent” merely because completed appointments prove harder to demonstrate.

**Source correction.** If a corrected capability statement establishes offline receipt in a newer release, reopen B's current eligibility judgment for that release; confirm the remaining workflow and transition conditions. Preserve the old failed-fit episode. Identify supplied audience or motion claims requiring revalidation without claiming those documents or the account were updated.

## Supplied account comparisons and arithmetic

Comparing deidentified cases is permissible analysis when the assignment and supplied data allow it. Keep the source snapshot and entity key, avoid unnecessary personal details, and describe analytical rule membership rather than writing sales-qualified labels. A list of analyzed IDs is not an authorized contact list.

Before counting, check whether rows represent accounts, users or episodes; whether one account appears in several source families; whether definitions and observation windows match; and whether the file is a complete frame or a selected extract. Preserve overlaps that reflect genuinely different jobs. Missing event rows do not prove no exposure or no activity.

Execute only approved local calculations with permitted tools. Report the actual source, inputs, unit and calculation performed. If execution is unavailable, provide a proposed formula or clearly labeled reported value—not a claim of verified arithmetic. Arithmetic on a selected sample does not cure its selection bias. Do not infer market prevalence, relative segment size or population success rates from these illustrative cases or from similarly selected real cases.

## Useful output before research exists

From product and job context alone, propose a conditional initial situation that could plausibly receive the intended progress. Name the current alternative, the decisive adoption prerequisite and a plausible competing situation. Explain what is assumption and what the supplied capability supports.

For example: “A provisional starting audience is people performing the supplied job through the supported workflow who can obtain the required input without extra integration work. Those needing assisted preparation may fit a separate combined offer if support is available. The first useful proof is whether a recent normal job reaches its intended outcome with acceptable effort, not whether the concept sounds appealing.”

This is useful without a completed ICP. It does not establish a chosen commercial audience or justify invented demographics, precision or a forced winner. If the product and job are missing, keep the outline conditional and ask the one question needed to ground it.
