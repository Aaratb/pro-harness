# Customer evidence and segments

Use sanitized/deidentified supplied accounts to explain reported experience, not to manufacture population statistics. Read each permitted account completely; missing information remains unknown. Do not collect or persist names, raw customer records or sensitive quotations.

Summarize the customer's context, current solution, job/desired outcome, likes, friction/workarounds, importance and qualitative satisfaction, notable evidence and contradictory accounts. Use plain language and exact safe source locators. Suggest scoped next questions with owner only when useful; do not contact customers or write artifacts from a worker.

When differences matter, group by supported behavior, needs, motivation and job context rather than demographics alone. Describe use cases/frequency/depth, journey, friction, current product fit, desired value and missing representation. Check whether segment definitions are coherent, distinguishable and useful; overlapping membership needs explicit accounting, not forced exclusivity. Validate against supplied aggregate usage where possible.

Segment sizes and prevalence require measured denominators; no minimum segment count, invented percentage, attributed quote or claim that a few interviews establish causality. Retain contradictory and underrepresented accounts. A reported problem is an observed account; suspected implementation defects require Review first. Recommend the smallest authorized aggregate or qualitative follow-up that could distinguish explanations. Investment/prioritization options need business context and opportunity costs, not automatic product changes.
