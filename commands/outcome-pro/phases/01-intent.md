# Phase 1 — Intent

Recover accepted PRD/requirements, original measurement plan, owner/decision/horizon and relevant release or campaign records. Record the customer problem, intended behavior change and value hypothesis. User-provided goals are allowed but must be labeled retrospective unless pre-intervention provenance exists. Unknown ownership, targets and sample/maturity policy remain unknown; no universal sample-size rule.

Select the method from `skills/outcome-pro/references/analytical-methods.md`:

- **Descriptive/milestone:** establish what exists/happened, for whom and when. No accepted comparison is required for a sourced fact. Human brief only; explicitly say comparative target achievement and causal impact were not assessed.
- **Comparative intervention:** recover original primary outcome, target, baseline, definition/unit, eligible and exposed population, guardrails, sample/maturity policy and windows. Required missing inputs remain insufficient, not a reason to rename the question descriptive.
- **Experiment:** recover actual accepted concurrent-arm design and planned decision rule. Human brief with design validity, uncertainty, practical value and guardrails. Never relabel a concurrent control arm as a historical baseline to fit report@1. A separate valid historical observed-target comparison may additionally use report@1, clearly scoped apart.

Keep a business/product primary outcome present even if its measurement is `null`; proxies/enablers remain secondary or accepted guardrail rows. For explicitly engineering/marketing/operations-only scope, its domain measure can be primary and downstream business impact is **not assessed**. Do not rename metrics to produce a positive verdict.

Separate delivery, exposure, adoption and benefit; release does not prove feature-flag exposure, staged-rollout reach or customer value. Missing linkage/exposure prevents overall intervention-success claims while permitting a sourced delivery fact. Non-code initiatives use their documented campaign/launch milestone in report `release` fields and initiative in `feature.name`; repository identity is not applicable when absent. No invented Git release or intervention for general business health.

For a value question, sketch the intended mechanism: enabling work → exposure → changed customer behavior → benefit, marking supported and missing links. This is a value chain to test, not causal proof. Keep a descriptive question direct; do not force revenue, all departments, or a fixed scorecard. Keep original intent immutable; prospective revisions belong to Phase 4.

Complete with selected mode, accepted scope/targets, provenance and explicit unknowns. Continue to evidence unless only this phase was selected.
