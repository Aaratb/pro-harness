# Phase 6 — Audit Certification

Re-read every material cited evidence record from the immutable snapshot. Validate state, policy, lane reports, findings, evidence, artifact digests, legal transitions, budgets, redaction, and mandatory lane dispositions.

Check that target and fitness claims have the required measurement type. Modeled evidence cannot prove current health. A probe error, blocked mandatory lane, unresolved high-impact dependency, budget degradation, or disputed one-way premise prevents a `SOUND` verdict.

Certification outcomes are `SOUND`, `CONDITIONAL`, `UNSOUND`, or `BLOCKED`. Record exact unmet controls and next safe actions. For Mixed mode, only the certified audit packet may constrain Phase 7. For Audit mode, proceed to remediation handoff assembly after user acceptance of consequences and residual risk.
