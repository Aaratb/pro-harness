# Phase 4 — Independent Verification

Verify critical and high-impact findings first. For each domain, launch a fresh capability-equivalent instance that did not author the finding. Provide only the claim, scope, immutable snapshot, and canonical evidence—not the original conclusion or persuasive narrative.

The verifier attempts to confirm or refute the claim with stronger evidence. Record `CONFIRMED`, `REFUTED`, `DISPUTED`, or `UNVERIFIED`; preserve the original record and dissent. A generic reviewer cannot erase a specialist-owned finding without capability-equivalent evidence.

Batch related findings within the verification-call budget. Conformance arbitrates cross-boundary disputes and the coordinated security owner arbitrates cross-focus duplication. Any material probe requirement becomes a bounded specification and remains unexecuted without separate consent.

Return the verified finding set, unresolved disputes, evidence changes, budget use, and certification blockers.
