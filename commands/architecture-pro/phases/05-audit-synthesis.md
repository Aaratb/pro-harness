# Phase 5 — Audit Synthesis

Deduplicate findings while preserving provenance. When three or more confirmed symptoms share an upstream missing boundary, invariant, contract, or owner, promote that cause as the structural finding and retain the symptoms as evidence and impacts.

For every material quality attribute, report current health, target attainment, and evidence confidence separately. A healthy-looking system can still have insufficient evidence; a well-evidenced system can still miss its target.

Rank structural findings and remediation directions by impact, dependency order, reversibility, risk reduction, and complexity cost. Record accepted edge cases, residual risk, owners, deadlines, and the evidence that would change the conclusion.

Present findings, root-cause clusters, consequence chains, grade recommendations, unknowns, and proposed remediation order for user correction before certification.
