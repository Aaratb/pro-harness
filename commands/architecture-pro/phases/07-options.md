# Phase 7 — Requirements and Architecture Options

Recover settled measurable quality targets, hard constraints, non-goals, current-state constraints, owners, risk threshold, and complexity budget; keep unresolved priorities provisional. In Mixed mode, import only the certified audit packet.

Load `plan-engineering-review` for architectural reasoning, not implementation-task generation. Translate decision-driving goals into quality scenarios: actor or stimulus, operating environment, required response, and response measure. Work through normal use, the most consequential failure, and likely change pressure where relevant; do not manufacture a scenario quota. If a business trade remains open, load `grill-with-docs` and ask a focused question before treating an assumption as a constraint.

Inventory existing services, modules, contracts, stores, queues, caches, deployment primitives, and libraries. Prefer reuse, then the smallest extension, before new machinery. Every new service, datastore, queue, framework, provider, or control plane requires an evidenced capability gap, owner, operational cost, and exit path.

When user-owned priorities remain open, load `architecture-explanation-diagrams` and show provisional rendered shapes before detailed comparison. Discuss the business consequence that separates them using `grill-with-docs`: for example, immediate completion versus a visible pending result, not merely a queue brand. Invite a different shape or constraint and incorporate the answer before investing in dependent detail. Reuse these views as the options mature; settled choices need no repeat interview. This early conversation is not final option selection or a substitute for independent challenge.

Compare the credible viable options, including reuse or the boring baseline where it satisfies hard constraints. Evaluate simplicity-first, scale-first, and evolvability-first lenses under the routing contract. Do not manufacture straw alternatives or pad the option count. With fewer than three viable options, record `option_scope`: why the set is narrow, rejected alternatives with forcing constraints and evidence references, and a fresh independent challenge with its lane-report and challenger instance identifiers. That challenger must examine the exclusions and narrowed set, not merely endorse the author. Preserve dissent and resolve material objections before selecting. If no option is viable, stop blocked. With one viable option, expose the forcing constraints and consequences rather than pretending a competitive score establishes superiority.

For APIs, CLIs, SDKs, events, configuration, or agent-facing surfaces, score discoverability, naming, defaults, errors, compatibility, migration, and measurable time to first success. When AI is material, include model, prompt, context, retrieval, tool, evaluation, safety, privacy, cost, latency, fallback, drift, and rollback contracts.

Load `architecture-explanation-diagrams`. Before requesting approval, show rendered candidate shapes with a compact component/data-flow view and the decision-sensitive sequence, not technology names alone. Reuse the existing option Markdown packet and its local HTML companion. Label changes on nodes and relationships against the Phase 2 baseline; a single viable option or ADR-only still needs a legible view of its consequences. Explain responsibility and state ownership, what stays together or crosses a process boundary, the dominant failure/coupling cost, and what existing code is reused. Options must differ in a meaningful architectural choice, not cosmetic stack substitutions. State the recommendation and strongest independent objection in plain language; keep detailed proof in the existing option packet.

Present the jointly refined options, constraints, unknowns, and proposed criteria weights totaling 100. Explain weights through the user's priorities; offer a justified calibration instead of requiring the user to invent numbers. Freeze this baseline only after approval, carrying prior explicit approvals forward when unchanged. Wait for explicit approval or edits before Phase 8.
