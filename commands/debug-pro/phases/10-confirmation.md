# Phase 10 — Confirmation & Independent Challenge

Confirm the exact mechanism at the origin with executed evidence appropriate to the defect. For ambiguous, high-risk or flaky cases, use the causal reference: necessity/sufficiency counterfactuals, magnitude/timing fit, adequate trials, and actively refuted material rivals.

A simple deterministic defect may use its exact reproduction and discriminating origin check. Do not impose a score formula or mandatory multi-agent exercise. For material uncertainty, use a fresh `system-diagnostics-analyst` (or the routing fallback) with the claim and cited evidence; load `architecture-diagnostics` when localization is the disputed question. Ask for the strongest counterevidence, a credible rival prediction, and what observation would change the conclusion. The read-only challenger proposes; the orchestrator performs any authorized probe. Record actual runtime identity, decisive evidence and whether challenge changed, narrowed or sustained the explanation in `DEBUG_REPORT.md`.

If delegation is unavailable, explicit self-critique and an executed rival prediction may strengthen the causal evidence, but are not independent review. Disclose missing independence; do not waive a required independent check or Phase 12's actual repair reviewer. Missing decisive causal evidence still blocks confirmation.

Separate trigger, latent condition, contributing factors and root cause where relevant. Never end the explanation at a person. Confirm expected behavior; disputed intent is a decision request, not a patch opportunity.

Gate: cause is qualitatively `CONFIRMED` for its explicit scope, or repair is blocked with the appropriate honest terminal status. No confidence number is generated.
