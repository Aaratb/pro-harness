# Phase 7 — Execution View & Handoff

Read `roadmap-execution-planning` and its capacity reference for dependencies or time placement. Preserve the exact human-selected set. Without established selection, label a useful scenario hypothetical; do not accept it by scheduling it.

Trace the complete customer outcome and required capabilities. A deferred or unselected enabler remains a human decision, not free work. Separate sourced dependencies, inferred constraints, preferred order and shared-resource collisions. Preserve all selected items, including blocked or unfinished work.

Use supplied role availability, calendars, estimates and concurrency constraints. Distinguish effort from elapsed time and nominal staffing from qualified capacity. A demand/capacity lower bound is not a feasible schedule: show an actual resource/window witness including trailing QA or other required readiness. Unknown cross-functional work is not free. Do not assign people, assume overtime or invent estimates to fill cells.

Compare decision-relevant sequencing alternatives and re-evaluate the limiting constraint after corrections. Keep pre-PRD dates provisional. New consequential feasibility claims need a scoped independent challenge before claiming the plan complete, not a rerun of every phase.

Finish with selected IDs and notes, supported sequence, unresolved dependencies/capacity, source/decision context, actual coverage and next owner. Recommend Feature Pro for the chosen new capability, Architecture Pro for structural decisions, Debug Pro for defects, or Review Pro for completed work. Recommendations do not execute commands or freeze a PRD. Offer the existing static HTML companion only when useful/requested; validate it without creating a new review application.
