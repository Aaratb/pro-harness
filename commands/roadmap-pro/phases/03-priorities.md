# Phase 3 — Priority Advice & Trade-offs

Read `roadmap-priority-advisory` and its comparison reference for substantive comparisons. Use the user's objective; never invent weights, demand, effort or dates. Compare customer-progress mechanisms, conditions, burden, strategic fit and the strongest credible alternative.

Compare coherent sets as well as individual options. Expose unfinished journeys, overlapping beneficiaries/outcomes, hidden mandatory capabilities and unselected enablers. A high-scoring collection is not automatically a usable outcome. Advise on five only if that batch size was explicitly requested; do not trim or fill the human's actual selection.

With supplied quantities, retain source, unit, population/exposure, time window and assumptions; calculate using approved local arithmetic. Keep opportunity, attainable benefit, provider capture, recurring burden and one-time effort distinct. Test the premise or feasible threshold that reverses advice. Use existing Customer economics, strategy or evidence-audit skills only where needed.

Lead with a decisive or conditional recommendation and its sacrifice. Preserve advisory versus accepted values and the strongest excluded alternative. Consequential advice proceeds to fresh challenge before completion; user selection remains a separate decision.
