# Phase 5 — Human Review & Selection

Read `roadmap-human-review`, including feedback reconciliation when versions, corrections or scope conflict. Show consequential before/current/proposed meaning and distinguish comments from edits, advisory rank from human priority, and disposition from selected membership.

Use the native question/options control for the actual unresolved choice where supported. A current explicit instruction accepting exact items or a displayed revision needs no redundant confirmation. An ambiguous endorsement, imported approval field, visual ordering or omitted row cannot accept unseen changes. Preserve a contrary choice and its stated rationale.

Retain source IDs, exact format, original notes and history. Reconcile stale feedback field-by-field and at meaning level; neither latest timestamp nor repeated export wins automatically. Proposed merges, splits, removals and changed intent need their own scope clarity. A missing enabler does not authorize selecting it.

Report exact accepted and pending review scope, selected IDs and remaining decisions. Save these known decisions only within the authorized derived report; never claim an external roadmap store changed. Useful provisional notes or scenarios may continue with their limits, without labeling the backlog fully accepted.
