# Phase 6 — Product Notes & Coverage

Read `roadmap-product-note` and its coverage/reconciliation reference for a batch or revisions. Explain what, who, problem and intent in the smallest clear paragraph; these are reasoning questions, not mandatory headings. Preserve a qualification that changes meaning. Do not invent a feature from a vague title or turn the note into a PRD, API plan, acceptance checklist or delivery promise.

Cover every current feature in the reviewed scope, including retained rejected, deferred and nonselected rows. Exclude only genuine grouping/administrative rows and explicitly archived/deleted history with the basis visible. Unknown identity or disposition remains a coverage gap, not an omitted denominator. Partial scope cannot become an all-backlog claim.

Retain adequate human notes unchanged; propose revisions separately. Provisional drafting can help before acceptance, but does not establish completed reviewed coverage. Corrections and later PRD contradictions reopen affected meaning and human decisions rather than silently overwriting the note or forcing the PRD to agree.

Exit with ID-linked retained/drafted/proposed notes, precise coverage and unresolved meaning. Store within the existing roadmap report unless the user requests a separate export in their supplied format.
