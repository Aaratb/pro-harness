# Phase 7 — Strategy & Validation

Use `customer-strategy-synthesis` for strategic choices and `customer-solution-validation` for study design or supplied-result assessment. `ab-test-analysis` remains conditional on a genuine experiment. Read the selected methods' relevant references; neither a complete preceding workflow nor a prototype is mandatory.

A strategic thesis must connect customer learning, ICP/roles, promise, actual/proposed mechanism, alternatives, adoption and economics. Explain why it could exist, win and sustain, the sacrifices involved, a credible counterstrategy and what would falsify it. Data, brand or a feature lead is not automatically a defensible advantage. Preserve service, partnership, existing-capability and no-entry alternatives.

For validation, distinguish designing a study from interpreting actual results. Choose a method that discriminates consequential hypotheses; predeclare sourced or justified interpretation criteria without arbitrary sample sizes, scores or conversion thresholds. Keep neutral discovery separate from concept exposure and track which stimulus/version participants saw. An understood or liked message is not demonstrated demand; a revised message has not inherited old validation.

Return a proposed thesis or bounded assessment with supporting/conflicting proof and the next decision. No study execution, GTM, pricing approval, roadmap order, resources, architecture or implementation is authorized. Send consequential conclusions to Phase 8's fresh challenge, not directly to build.
