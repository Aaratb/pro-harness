# Phase 6 — Customer Fit & Value

Load only relevant methods: `customer-icp-definition` for fit, `customer-market-intelligence` for actual alternatives, `customer-opportunity-economics` for value/capture, and `customer-positioning` for the promise and foundational messages. A focused ICP or positioning request does not require a market-sizing exercise.

Explain the fit mechanism: customer situation/job → current or proposed capability → attainable progress relative to alternatives → adoption, buying and serveability conditions. Distinguish account, person and role; best-fit, poor-fit and unknown conditions; current fit versus future conditional fit. A candidate ICP must not exclude research counterexamples or approve commercial targeting.

Compare customer choices, including manual/service/no-change alternatives, on consequential criteria. Vendor silence is not absent capability; feature parity is not demonstrated demand. For economics separate eligible exposure, attainable customer benefit, provider capture, delivery costs and overlap. Execute supported arithmetic, expose reversals, and treat 10x/100x as scenarios rather than multiplied forecasts.

Draft useful positioning and role-sensitive message hypotheses tied to actual capability and evidence. Keep a proposed promise visibly provisional; no invented uniqueness, endorsements or measured outcomes. Clarity, liking, need, adoption and payment are different claims. New current-source verification returns to authorized Phase 2, never a worker's silent browse.

Return the requested fit/value/positioning thesis with counterevidence, unknowns and next proof. Consequential recommendations proceed to fresh challenge; no fit or commercial outcome is certified by a completed card.
