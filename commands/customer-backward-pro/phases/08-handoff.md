# Phase 8 — Challenge & Handoff

Use `customer-independent-challenge` through a fresh `customer-evidence-analyst` for an explicit challenge request or a consequential fit, strategy, population/economic or validation recommendation. Use `customer-evidence-audit` only for unresolved evidence fitness. A narrow script draft or note summary does not acquire an automatic review panel.

The challenger receives approved original sources, question/constraints and candidate—not private author rationale or previous verdicts. Independently reconstruct load-bearing quantities and interpretations. Test the strongest contrary explanation, missing voices, proxy/denominator changes, source dependence, concept contamination, current/future capability, switching, capture and scale only where relevant. Preserve defensible claims; do not manufacture objections or treat agreement as additional evidence.

The coordinator answers the critique, revises affected authorized derived conclusions and preserves consequential dissent. One bounded critique/response/synthesis is the default. Missing originals or compromised actor freshness limit the claim; unavailable required independence needs a user waiver or an awaiting-challenge status, not a simulated review.

Deliver the strongest supported answer, source and calculation evidence, what changed or remained unchanged, limits/falsifiers and the next specific owner decision. Record actual phase/method/challenge coverage and validate any persisted trace once. Completion refers to the requested research work, never proof of market fit or study execution. Retain no-new-product and insufficient-evidence outcomes.

Offer, but do not dispatch, the appropriate existing downstream workflow after the owner's decision. Do not invent an uninstalled roadmap command or translate research attention into delivery priority. Close with a concise usable handoff, not eight repeated phase reports.
