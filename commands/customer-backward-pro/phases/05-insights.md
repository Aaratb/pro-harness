# Phase 5 — Behavior & Insights

Select `customer-quantitative-analysis`, `customer-behavior-synthesis` or `customer-insight-synthesis` for the actual question; do not preload all three. Use `ab-test-analysis` only for a genuine documented experiment. The Customer quantitative reference owns cohort, sampling and causal interpretation; existing query/measurement leaf references are optional, not an Outcome run.

Quantitative work can originate questions before interviews. Check units, eligibility/exposure, observation age, joins, missingness, censoring and relevant segment/mix contrasts. Execute material calculations through the authorized arithmetic role or coordinator and retain inputs, formula, output and inference limits. Unknown is not zero; observational change is not causal lift.

Reconstruct whole-job episodes across product boundaries: trigger, actors, choices, handoffs, workarounds, result and consequence. Explain why non-use or the current alternative can be rational. Situational profiles need observable membership evidence, not invented biographies or a fixed persona count.

Synthesize mechanisms rather than renamed themes: supporting/conflicting episodes → interpretation → strongest rival → prediction → changed or retained belief → next discriminator. Keep source families independent and selected cases distinct from population claims. Locate awareness/access, service/process, incentive, reliability and no-gap alternatives before proposing a missing capability.

Return supported learning and honest non-findings. Reopen only affected claims when definitions or evidence change; do not keep a contradicted headline alive with a footnote. No roadmap or product change follows automatically.
