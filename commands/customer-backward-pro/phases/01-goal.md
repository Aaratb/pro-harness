# Phase 1 — Goal & Current Beliefs

Use `customer-research-framing`; apply inline unless an independent framing question is useful. Identify the customer/business decision, what research might change and what would happen without it. Ask only for the unresolved choice that changes research direction; missing evidence is a valid starting state.

Keep sponsor preference, existing ICP/positioning and constraints as sourced beliefs, not customer truth. Identify users, buyers, payers, gatekeepers and whole-job situations only where relevant. Start from recent behavior and consequences, not endorsement of a proposed feature. Name consequential rival explanations and the next observation that could distinguish them. Confirming the existing view, no unmet need and no change are valid.

Return a useful brief or update the corresponding section of `research.md`: decision, current beliefs, source/assumption status, missing perspectives and next learning. A provisional brief is not a validated problem or authority for acquisition. Continue to the next relevant phase; do not demand completed research to formulate the question.
