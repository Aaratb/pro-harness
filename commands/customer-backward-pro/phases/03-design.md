# Phase 3 — Research Design

Load `customer-cohort-design` for sampling/selection questions and `customer-interview-design` for instruments. Choose only the relevant method; a request for an interview script does not require a new cohort study. Inspect current questions and evidence first. Quantitative patterns can inform contrasts without establishing motives.

Separate target population, accessible frame, analytical cohort and recruitment cohort. Test proxies, entity/exposure windows, overlap and missing voices. Select cases for learning, not convenient endorsements; purposive interviews are not prevalence estimates. Preserve opt-outs, unknown contactability and selection provenance without creating a contact list or outreach operation.

Design neutral recent-episode probes with stable question IDs, role/context branches, short mode, negative cases and separate concept exposure. Keep buyer/operator knowledge distinct. New or materially changed instruments are candidates; only inspected field evidence supports field-tested status for specified roles, languages and questions. Specify permission/readiness needs without pretending consent or calls occurred.

Return the usable instrument or sampling design, version/comparability limits and next research action. If this was the requested outcome, finish here with its limits; do not fabricate downstream fieldwork or force an eight-stage run.
